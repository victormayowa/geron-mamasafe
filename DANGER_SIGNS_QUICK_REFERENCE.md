# 🚨 Danger Signs Quick Reference Card

## 🔴 RED - EMERGENCY! Go to Hospital IMMEDIATELY

### MOTHERS
- ✅ Heavy vaginal bleeding
- ✅ Severe abdominal pain
- ✅ Convulsions/seizures
- ✅ Severe headache with blurred vision
- ✅ Difficulty breathing
- ✅ Reduced/absent baby movements
- ✅ Water breaking before labor
- ✅ Thoughts of harming self/baby

### NEWBORNS (0-28 days)
- ✅ Not feeding at all
- ✅ Convulsions
- ✅ Fast breathing (>60 breaths/min)
- ✅ Severe chest indrawing
- ✅ Fever (≥37.5°C)
- ✅ Body too cold (<35.5°C)
- ✅ Yellow palms and soles
- ✅ Unconscious/floppy

### INFANTS (1-12 months)
- ✅ Unable to drink/breastfeed
- ✅ Convulsions
- ✅ Fast breathing (≥50/min)
- ✅ Severe chest indrawing
- ✅ Persistent vomiting

### CHILDREN (1-5 years)
- ✅ Unable to drink
- ✅ Convulsions
- ✅ Fast breathing (≥40/min)
- ✅ Unconscious/lethargic
- ✅ Severe dehydration

### ADOLESCENTS (10-19 years)
- ✅ Thoughts of suicide/self-harm
- ✅ Severe abdominal pain (if sexually active)
- ✅ Difficulty breathing
- ✅ Substance overdose

---

## 🟡 YELLOW - Visit Health Center TODAY

### MOTHERS
- ✅ Persistent vomiting
- ✅ Fever (>38°C)
- ✅ Painful urination
- ✅ Mild swelling of legs
- ✅ Foul-smelling discharge (postpartum)
- ✅ Red, swollen breast

### NEWBORNS
- ✅ Umbilical cord infection

### INFANTS
- ✅ Diarrhea with blood
- ✅ High fever

### CHILDREN
- ✅ Severe malnutrition signs
- ✅ Severe paleness
- ✅ Swollen belly with edema

### ADOLESCENTS
- ✅ Severe depression/anxiety
- ✅ Abnormal vaginal bleeding

---

## 🟢 GREEN - Home Care is SAFE

### MOTHERS
- ✅ Mild nausea (first trimester)
- ✅ Mild headache
- ✅ Mild back pain
- ✅ Mood swings (baby blues)

### ALL PATIENTS
- ✅ Mild cold symptoms
- ✅ Normal fatigue
- ✅ Mild menstrual cramps (adolescents)

---

## 📞 What to Do

### 🔴 RED Signs:
1. **DO NOT WAIT**
2. Go to hospital IMMEDIATELY
3. Have someone drive you
4. Do NOT drive yourself
5. Lie on left side (if mother)

### 🟡 YELLOW Signs:
1. Visit health center TODAY
2. Do not delay
3. Monitor for worsening
4. Follow home care instructions

### 🟢 GREEN Signs:
1. Home care is safe
2. Follow care instructions
3. Monitor symptoms
4. Visit clinic if worsens
